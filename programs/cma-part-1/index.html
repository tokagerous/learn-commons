Official IMA references

  • This program's structure follows the IMA's 2024 CMA Content Specification Outlines (effective Sep 1, 2024).
  • Exam mechanics, eligibility, and scoring policies: CMA Handbook.
  • Sections A, B, C are the deepest (built first). Sections D, E, F are newly scaffolded — fewer questions per topic but every CSO sub-topic is covered.

How each section works

  • Each unit has a plain-language explanation, a visual diagram or table, an interactive (drag-drop or calculator), and 2-3 inline quizzes with try-again and per-distractor explanations.
  • Inline quizzes don't penalise — wrong clicks eliminate that option and reveal a rationale; you can keep trying.
  • Section tests run a CMA-pace timer (1.8 min/question), let you flag questions, and offer "Replay missed only" or "Replay flagged only" after grading.
  • Highlight any text to mark it (4 colours), attach notes, and bookmark units. All saved per-browser.
  • Press / to search, F for the formula panel, ? for keyboard help.
Open-source, no tracking, no signup. Progress and annotations live in your browser's localStorage. Switch devices and you'll start fresh on that device.
