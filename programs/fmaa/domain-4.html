Unit 01 · Domain 4

Direct material variances

Price and quantity decomposition

Practice 1 — DM price variance

Standard 4 lbs/unit at $5/lb. Actual: produced 800 units, used 3,400 lbs at $4.80/lb. The DM PRICE variance is:

  • $680 F
  • $680 U
  • $1,000 F
  • $1,000 U
Why: Price variance = (Actual price − Standard price) × Actual quantity = ($4.80 − $5.00) × 3,400 = −$680 → $680 favourable (paid LESS than standard).

Practice 2 — DM quantity variance

Same scenario: Standard 4 lbs/unit; produced 800 units. Used 3,400 lbs. The DM QUANTITY variance:

  • $0
  • $200 F
  • $1,000 F
  • $1,000 U
Why: Standard quantity for output = 800 × 4 = 3,200 lbs. Quantity variance = (Actual qty − Standard qty) × Standard price = (3,400 − 3,200) × $5 = $1,000 unfavourable. Used 200 lbs more than the standard allows for the production achieved.

Practice 3 — Responsibility center types

A division manager evaluated on operating profit but with no authority over capital investment is operating which type of responsibility center?

  • Cost center
  • Revenue center
  • Profit center
  • Investment center
Why: Profit center = revenue + cost authority but NOT investment authority. Investment center managers additionally control invested capital and are evaluated on ROI / RI / EVA. Match the metric to the authority.
