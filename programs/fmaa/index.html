FMAA — visual field guide.

An entry-level credential from the IMA for students and early-career professionals. Half the depth of the CMA, but it covers the same conceptual ground — financial-statement basics, cost concepts, planning and budgeting fundamentals, performance analysis, and professional ethics. This guide walks you through all five sections with diagrams, practice questions, and exam-pace tests.

About the FMAA exam

80 multiple-choice questions · 2 hours · computer-based at Prometric centers worldwide.

Five sections per the IMA's official 2024 CSO: A 25%, B 25%, C 20%, D 20%, E 10%. Cognitive levels A and B only (no Level C — that's the CMA's territory).

Eligibility: open to anyone — no degree or experience prerequisite. Designed as a stepping-stone to the CMA. Once you pass, you can list "FMAA" after your name and use it on your resume immediately.
