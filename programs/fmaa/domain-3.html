Unit 01 · Domain 3

The master budget — overview

What it is and how its pieces fit together

Practice 1 — Master budget driver

Which schedule is built FIRST in the master budget?

  • Production budget
  • Cash budget
  • Sales budget
  • Income statement
Why: Sales budget drives every other operating budget. Production depends on sales (and inventory policy); DM/DL/MOH depend on production. Cash and budgeted statements are aggregations near the end.

Practice 2 — Static vs. flexible

For evaluating a manager's performance, the BEST budget tool is:

  • Static budget set at year-start
  • Master budget income statement
  • Flexible budget recast at actual production volume
  • Capital budget
Why: Static budgets fix volume in advance — comparing actual to static mixes volume effect with price/efficiency. Flexible budgets recast at actual volume, isolating the variances the manager actually controlled. Always evaluate against flexible.

Practice 3 — Production budget

Sales 10,000 units. Beginning FG 800; desired ending FG 1,200. Production required:

  • 9,600
  • 10,000
  • 10,400
  • 12,000
Why: Production = Sales + Desired Ending FG − Beginning FG = 10,000 + 1,200 − 800 = 10,400. The 9,600 trap reverses ending and beginning. Don't confuse direction.
