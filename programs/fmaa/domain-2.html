Unit 01 · Domain 2

Cost classifications — variable vs. fixed

What changes with volume vs. what doesn't

Practice 1 — Variable cost behaviour

A company's electricity bill rises with machine hours run. This cost is BEST classified as:

  • Variable cost
  • Fixed cost
  • Period cost
  • Sunk cost
Why: Variable costs change in direct proportion to activity (machine hours, units produced). Fixed costs are constant regardless of activity within the relevant range. Period costs (S&A) are time-based, not activity-based. Sunk costs are past, irrelevant to future decisions.

Practice 2 — CVP break-even

A product sells for $50, has variable cost of $30, and fixed costs are $80,000. Break-even units:

  • 8,000
  • 4,000
  • 2,667
  • 1,600
Why: Contribution margin per unit = $50 − $30 = $20. Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ CM per unit = $80,000 ÷ $20 = 4,000 units. The trap: dividing by price ($50) gives 1,600 — wrong because variable costs eat into each unit's contribution.

Practice 3 — Contribution margin ratio

Same product (price $50, VC $30). Contribution margin RATIO is:

  • 60%
  • 30%
  • 40%
  • 20%
Why: CM ratio = CM per unit ÷ Price = $20 ÷ $50 = 40%. CM ratio is the fraction of each sales dollar that contributes to fixed costs and profit. Useful for break-even revenue: BE revenue = Fixed costs ÷ CM ratio = $80,000 ÷ 40% = $200,000.
